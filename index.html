Hi!

I am a Computer Engineer with Master's degree in Computational Intelligence and Internet of Things. I am committed to making a positive impact on the planet and to enhance my skills and continue on the research path.

Throughout my academic career, I have explored the intersection between Computer Vision and ecology, improving my skills in AI and data analysis while gaining knowledge of ecological needs. I have worked with mammal camera trap images and bird acoustic data, both in the Doñana area, enriching my experience in biodiversity conservation and the management and analysis of different types of data.

Currently, I am involved in projects focused on automated biodiversity monitoring using Deep Learning techniques. I am dedicated to developing solutions for wildlife monitoring and conservation, as well as improving existing tools for specific cases. My goal is to use my expertise in AI to drive improvements in environmental conservation initiatives.
